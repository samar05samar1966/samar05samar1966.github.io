BEYOND COMPUTER SCIENCE

My Passions

Mentorship

Over the past three years, I have been deeply committed to mentoring youth and supporting others in my community. My “Your Guide to College” website has helped over 70 students successfully apply to college and prepare for the transition into higher education. As a first-generation college student, mentorship holds a special place in my heart because it allows me to share the lessons I’ve learned, helping others avoid the challenges I once faced.

Self-development

Personal growth is one of my core values. I believe that every experience, whether academic, professional, or personal, offers a new opportunity to learn. My diverse experiences, from internships to community involvement, have shaped the way I approach challenges, encouraging me to stay curious and adaptable.

Creativity

Creativity is at the center of how I think, learn, and connect with others. It allows me to see challenges from multiple perspectives and approach problems with innovation and openness. Whether I’m designing a website, collaborating on a group project, or brainstorming new ideas, creativity helps me adapt to new environments and express myself confidently.
